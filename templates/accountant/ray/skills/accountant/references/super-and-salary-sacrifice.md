# Super and salary sacrifice

**Baseline FY2026–27.** Verify caps on the ATO.

## Headline numbers

| Item | FY2026–27 baseline |
| --- | --- |
| Super guarantee (SG) | 12% of ordinary time earnings |
| Payday Super | SG due with salary (from 1 July 2026) — not quarterly |
| Concessional cap | $30,000 in 2025–26; **$32,500** from 1 July 2026 |
| Non-concessional cap | $130,000 (nil if over transfer balance cap) |
| Division 293 | extra 15% on concessional contribs when income + concessional > **$250,000** |
| Max contributions base | ~$270,830 p.a. (SG stops accruing above this) |
| Tax on concessional contribs inside the fund | 15% (30% effective with Div 293) |

Concessional = employer SG + salary sacrifice + personal deductible
contributions.

## Headroom at this job

They currently pay **SG only — no extra contributions**.

OTE is likely the **$150,000 base**. Overtime (≥$10k) is usually **not**
OTE. Bonus (~$20k) — ask payroll. If only SG on $150k: 12% × $150,000 =
**$18,000**. Remaining concessional room FY2026–27 ≈
**$32,500 − $18,000 = $14,500**.

They want to stop work by **52**. These ~4 remaining PAYG years are the
practical window to use that room. After they resign, concessional
contributions from employment stop.

At a **39%** marginal rate (37% + 2% Medicare), $1 sacrificed to super is
taxed at 15% in the fund instead of 39% — about **24 cents in the dollar**
before considering HELP, MLS, or Div 293. That money is still locked
until **60**. Never describe salary sacrifice as spendable at 52.

Partner earns $0 — a **spouse super contribution** (after-tax) may be
worth mentioning; don't push it as a default.

Sacrifice **must be agreed with payroll before the pay is earned**. It
cannot be backdated at EOFY.

## Carry-forward unused cap

If total super balance was under the transfer-balance cap on 30 June of
the prior year, unused concessional cap from the previous five years may
be available. Ask before telling them they can only use this year's
$32,500.

## Division 293

Unlikely on the job alone. Job (~$180k+ with OT) + net rent + ETF
distributions + concessional contribs approaching $250k is the watch.

## Other packaging

Super is the usual high-income lever. Cars, devices, and other FBT
benefits depend on what the employer offers; FBT can eat the saving.
MoneySmart: https://moneysmart.gov.au/work-and-tax/salary-packaging

## HELP interaction

Salary sacrifice reduces taxable income, which can reduce HELP
repayments. Model both if they have a loan, especially near the $186,051
10% band.
