# Sydney investment property

Client: **one Sydney apartment bought 23 April 2004 for $385,000**, now
~$1,000,000, rent $880/week (≈ $45,760/year, gross yield ≈ 4.6%).
**Lived in it 23 Apr 2004 – Dec 2019**, rented since. Six-year absence
**ended Dec 2025.** **Mortgage paid off.** Ownership % with the partner
still to confirm.

## Occupancy (do this first)

| Use | Tax |
| --- | --- |
| Principal place of residence (they live there) | Generally no rental deductions; CGT main-residence exemption may apply (partial if it was ever rented) |
| Genuine rental (or genuinely available at market rates) | Rent is assessable; allowable expenses deductible |
| Mixed year (moved in/out) | Apportion by days and private use |

$880/week is a tenant. They lived there until **Dec 2019**; it is an
investment now. Do not re-ask occupancy or the move-out year.

This client's loan is **nil**, so skip interest. The unit is **positively
geared**: rent is assessable and only the remaining running costs (strata,
rates, water, insurance, agent, repairs, land tax, depreciation) offset it.
Their Brisbane rent ($1,420/week) is private and irrelevant to this
schedule.

## Immediate deductions (when it is a rental)

Claim in the year incurred, for the period it was rented or genuinely
available (ATO rental expenses):

- Interest on the investment loan (not principal)
- Body corporate / strata **admin** fund (sinking fund is often capital)
- Council rates, water, land tax, insurance (building, landlord)
- Agent fees and advertising
- Repairs and maintenance (not improvements)
- Pest, cleaning, gardening
- Borrowing costs — usually over 5 years if > $100

**Not deductible as they go:** stamp duty, conveyancing (cost base),
principal, travel to inspect (resident individuals, post-2017 rules),
most initial repairs that are capital.

Apportion if part private, part rented, or mixed-purpose loan.

## Capital vs repair

- Repair = restore existing function → immediate.
- Improve / replace with something better → capital (Div 43 or cost base).

## Depreciation

- **Div 43 capital works:** structure, usually 2.5% p.a. over 40 years for
  residential built after 15 Sep 1987. Needs construction cost (quantity
  surveyor).
- **Div 40 plant:** carpets, appliances, etc. **Second-hand plant** in
  residential premises acquired after 9 May 2017 is generally not
  depreciable. New items they buy can be.

Recommend a quantity-surveyor schedule if they don't have one. The fee is
usually deductible.

## Gearing

**This client has no loan.** Subtract strata, rates, insurance, agent
(~5–8% of rent), vacancy, maintenance, and land tax if any. What remains
of the $45,760 is **taxable rental profit**, added to the ~$180k job.

Do not talk about negative gearing unless they redraw or borrow against
the unit. MoneySmart still applies to cash flow: Brisbane rent they pay
($73,840/year) is a household cost, not a property deduction.
https://moneysmart.gov.au/property-investment/buying-an-investment-property

## CGT (when they sell)

Cost base starts at **$385,000 (23 Apr 2004)** plus stamp duty, legal,
and capital improvements. They **lived there until Dec 2019**.

**6-year absence rule** (dwelling used to produce income after they
moved out): they can keep treating it as the main residence for up to
six years after leaving, and they do not own another home. Rental
deductions during those years are still allowed.

- Moved out **December 2019** → absence window **ended December 2025**.
  As at Sep 2026 that clock has **already lapsed** (~9 months of
  post-absence days so far).
- Days **after Dec 2025** are the ones in the CGT net if they use the
  rule — a thin slice of ~22 years owned, not the whole ~$615k paper gain.
- Partial exemption: gain × (non-MR days / days owned). Then 50% discount
  on the taxable remainder if held > 12 months.
- Every extra year of holding as a rental now adds taxable days. Selling
  in 2026 vs waiting until 52 is a real CGT difference. Model before they
  resign if the unit is meant to fund 52–60.

ATO: main residence exemption after you move out / six-year absence.
Do not quote a full CGT bill on $615k. A December **day** is only needed
for a lodgement worksheet.

## NSW land tax

Assessed on **unimproved land value** (Valuer General), not the $1m sale
price. Strata land value is a share of the block and is often well below
$1m. Threshold (fixed from 2024): **general $1,075,000**, premium
$6,571,000. Rate: $100 + 1.6% above the general threshold.

- PPOR is generally exempt — **this unit is not their home**, so no
  main-residence land-tax exemption.
- Investment land is aggregated across NSW holdings at 31 December.
- A single Sydney unit often sits **under** the threshold — check the
  Valuer General land value, don't assume $1m market value.

https://www.revenue.nsw.gov.au/taxes-duties-levies-royalties/land-tax/understanding-land-tax/thresholds-and-rates
