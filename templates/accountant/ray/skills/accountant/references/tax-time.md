# Tax time

Australian income year: **1 July – 30 June**. This file is the lodgement
workflow. Ray prepares; a registered tax agent lodges.

## Dates (typical)

| When | What |
| --- | --- |
| 1 July | New year starts; prior year can be prepared |
| July–August | Income statements appear in myGov / ATO online |
| **31 October** | Due if **self-lodging** |
| Later (agent) | Registered agents have a lodgement program — often into the following year if the client is on their list |

If they already have an agent, don't panic them about 31 October.

## Pack for this client

**Income**

- myGov income statement (base $150k + bonus ~$20k + overtime ≥$10k, PAYG, SG)
- CommBank interest (annual statement / myGov prefill)
- ETF annual/AMMA statements for **VGE, VAS, NDQ, HYLD** (~$200k)
- Rental: $880/week × weeks let (no loan-interest statement — paid off)
- Crypto: only if BTC/ETH was sold, swapped, or spent (CGT worksheet)
- Other: RSUs, foreign, side work

**Work**

- WFH hours log
- Receipts for memberships, equipment, courses
- Car log only if they genuinely use a car for work (rare for office IT)

**Property (Sydney unit)**

- Agent annual statement or own ledger ($880/week × occupancy)
- **No loan interest** — mortgage is paid off
- Strata levies (admin vs sinking, if split)
- Council, water, insurance, land tax assessment
- Depreciation schedule (quantity surveyor) — still worth it with no loan
- Purchase contract + stamp duty (cost base — keep forever)

**Offsets / other**

- Private health: **Australian Unity** basic, mainly for MLS. Keep the
  annual statement (rebate + proof of hospital cover). Confirm the
  partner is on the policy.
- HELP balance
- Super: concessional total vs cap; notice of intent if claiming a personal
  deduction

## Order of work

1. Confirm the year being lodged vs the year being planned.
2. Occupancy of the apartment for that year.
3. Income statement + rental net.
4. Deductions with evidence.
5. Super cap and salary-sacrifice *for the year still open*.
6. Draft a one-page brief for the human agent: income, net rental, questions.

## What Ray will not do

- Lodge via myGov
- Apply a TFN, amend a return, or represent them to the ATO
- Tell them to claim a figure they cannot evidence
- Skip the disclaimer on a number that might be lodged
