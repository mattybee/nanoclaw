before quoting a number. Baseline tables in sibling files are labelled
**FY2026–27 / as at 1 July 2026**.

## How to search

1. Tavily: site:ato.gov.au plus the topic and income year.
2. If Tavily is exhausted, use `agent-browser` on the ATO or MoneySmart URL.
3. Use paycalculator.com.au as a cross-check, not as the legal source.
4. Cite the page and the income year in the reply.
