# Price compare — fair price, where to buy

Answer three things: **what is a fair price**, **where to buy**, and
**whether the shop can be trusted** (trust detail in
`trust-and-scams.md`).

## Effort by ticket

| Ticket | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Under ~$50 | One live check. Answer fast. |
| ~$50–$300 | Two or three AU shops + OzBargain. |
| $300+ | Cross-shop, reviews, warranty, pickup vs ship. |
| $1,000+ | Full depth. History if available. Name the catch. |

Open with a first impression from known ranges, then verify. Never leave
them waiting on a cheap item.

## Landed cost

Rank on **all-in AUD**, not the sticker.

- AU GST is already in the shelf price.
- Add shipping, or note free pickup in Brisbane.
- Overseas: shipping + GST/duty if it will be charged, plus wait.
- Membership gates (OnePass, eBay Plus, Amazon) are a catch — say so.
- Coupons only count if you confirmed they apply at cart. "Up to 70%
  off" and unused codes are not savings.

Timestamp every figure. Stock and price both rot.

## Fake discount

Do not trust RRP or a struck-through "was".

1. Same SKU at two other AU shops.
2. History: Camelcamelcamel AU, BuyWisely, OzBargain comments.
3. Unit price for consumables (per 100g, per litre, per roll).
4. Open-box / warehouse / marketplace vs sold-and-shipped by the retailer.

A "deal" that matches the everyday JB price is not a deal.

## Exact item

Don't compare a 512 GB Wi-Fi iPad to a 1 TB Cellular. Record:

- Brand + model + size/colour/capacity
- New vs refurb vs marketplace
- Seller (who is Sold by / Shipped by)

If two variants could be confused, ask one question.

## Verdict shape

```markdown
**Verdict: Fair — typical AU street is $310–$340, this is $329 at JB.**

| Buy from | Landed | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [JB Hi-Fi](https://...) | $329 pickup | AU warranty |
| [Amazon AU](https://...) | $318 + $0 ship | Marketplace seller — check returns |

Skip the $199 no-name listing — classic too-good-to-be-true.
```

Only quote prices you actually opened. Thin results: say so.
