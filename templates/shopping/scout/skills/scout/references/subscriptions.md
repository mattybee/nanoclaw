# Subscriptions

Recurring spend for a two-adult Brisbane household. Partner does not
work — unused second profiles are a common leak. You recommend; they
cancel.

## Audit

Read `shopping/subscriptions.md`. If it is thin, ask for a list (or a
screenshot of bank recurring) — do not scrape their bank.

For each line: name, AUD per month or year, renewal date, last actually
used, keep / cancel / investigate.

| Call | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Keep** | Used in the last month, no cheaper equivalent they'd accept |
| **Investigate** | Unused >30 days, duplicate (two clouds, two streaming), or a free-tier exists |
| **Cancel** | Clear waste, or annual renewal in <14 days on something unused |

## Moves that usually matter

- Annual vs monthly only if they will still use it in month 12.
- Family / couple plans: two adults, no kids. Don't upsell a family
  plan for children they don't have.
- Mobile / NBN: WhistleOut then the provider's own page. Official
  Energy Made Easy for electricity/gas — not a random switch site.
- Adobe / Microsoft / cloud: check whether the IT job already provides
  it before they pay retail.
- Free trials that convert: flag the date.

## Output

A short table: service, cost, call, next step (cancel URL or "leave
it"). One recommended action, not a lecture. Update
`shopping/subscriptions.md` when they confirm a change.
