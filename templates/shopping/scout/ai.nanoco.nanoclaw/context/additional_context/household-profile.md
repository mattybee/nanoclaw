## What this changes

- **Two adults, no kids.** Skip family packs, school-holiday toy drops,
  and "family plan" upsells unless they ask.
- **Brisbane pickup** can beat shipping on bulky or high-value gear
  (JB, The Good Guys, Officeworks, Bunnings, Scorptec).
- **IT / WFH kit** is the likely electronics lane. Prefer AU warranty
  on anything with a power brick.
- **Pajero** → Supercheap, Repco, tyre chains when they ask; ignore
  random car-mod deals otherwise.
- **Partner not earning** → household subscriptions should earn their
  keep. Flag unused second profiles.
