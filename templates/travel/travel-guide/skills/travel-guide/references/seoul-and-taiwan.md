## Entry

- **Korea:** Australian passports are **K-ETA exempt until 31 Dec 2026**.
  Tourist stay up to 90 days, no K-ETA and no visa. Optional e-Arrival card
  at www.e-arrivalcard.go.kr within 3 days of arrival (paper form still
  available on landing). Passport 6 months validity. Re-check after 31 Dec 2026.
- **Taiwan:** visa-exempt for Australians on a short stay; **TWAC** online
  within 3 days before arrival.

## Seoul — first two weeks (from Mon 7 Sep 2026)

Stay in **one walkable base**, not a hotel-hop. For this couple, prefer:

| Area | Why | Avoid if |
|------|-----|----------|
| Ikseon-dong / Jongno / Anguk | Hanok alleys, palaces, food, quiet at night | They want nightlife |
| Hannam / Itaewon-adjacent | Cafes, walking, Namsan, good apartments | Weekend Itaewon crowds |
| Seongsu / Seongsu-dong | Cafes, riverside, contemporary Seoul | They want palaces at the door |
| Yeonnam / Yeonhui | Residential, Gyeongui Line Forest | Too far from old-city days |

Hongdae is a night-out, not a 2-week base. Myeongdong is for a visit, not a stay.

### Rhythm for 14 unhurried days
- Days 1–2: light — jet lag from BNE (ICN, AREX or taxi to the stay)
- Cluster by district: Jongno palaces, then Bukchon/Ikseon, then Ikseong
  markets; a Seongsu day; a riverside / Banpo day; one day trip (Suwon or
  Ganghwado or a spa) not every other day
- One rest morning per 3–4 days
- Book 1–2 dinners (Korean BBQ with a room, or a reservation-only spot).
  Leave lunches to whatever's next door
- September: still warm, late typhoon / rain possible — indoor backup
  (cafes, baths, museums) for 2–3 days

### Money and getting around
- Cards work widely. Keep a little cash for markets.
- T-money on day one. Climate Card if they're on the subway most days.
- Directions: **Naver Map only** — see `korea-taiwan-maps`.
