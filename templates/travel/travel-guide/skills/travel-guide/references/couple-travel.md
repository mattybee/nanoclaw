# Couple travel (forties, no kids)

Default traveller profile unless `memory/preferences.md` says otherwise.

## Pace
- Unhurried: one standout thing in the morning, neighbourhood wander after
- First day after a long-haul is light — jet lag is real from BNE
- One rest morning per 3–4 days
- Skip park-hopper / kids-club / family-buffet itineraries

## Where to stay
- Boutique hotels, well-reviewed 4-star, or an apartment with a kitchen
- Walkable neighbourhoods over resort compounds
- Quiet room over a "view" that sits on a main road
- One splurge night if the trip is long enough to earn it

## Food
- Book 1–2 dinners that need a reservation; leave the rest open
- Markets, wine bars, long lunches — not playground-adjacent cafes
- Always ask dietary notes from preferences before picking restaurants

## From Brisbane
Good short hops: Sunshine Coast / Noosa, Gold Coast hinterland, Byron Bay,
Sydney, Melbourne, Hobart, Canberra, New Zealand.
Asia is the natural long-haul (direct-ish): Japan, Korea, Singapore, Bali
(check Smartraveller), Vietnam, Thailand. Europe is a two-stop winter project.

## What they are usually not after
- Theme parks, water parks, kids' clubs
- Pub crawls and hostel bar scenes
- Ultra-budget dorms
- 12-hour touring days with no sit-down meal

## Packing bias
- Walking shoes that are already worn in
- One outfit that works for a nicer restaurant
- Layers, not a separate "holiday wardrobe"
