## Where to save
- **Accommodation**: hostels/guesthouses > mid hotels > luxury
- **Food**: street food + self-catering > restaurants
- **Transport**: public transit > ride-share > taxis > rental
- **Attractions**: free walking tours, museum free days, city passes

## Where to splurge
- **Experiences** over things: a cooking class, guided trek, private tour
- **One nice meal** per trip — memory value is high
- **Location** of accommodation — central saves time+transit cost
- **Travel insurance** — never skip this

## Booking timing cheat sheet
- **Flights**: domestic 1-2 months out, international 3-6 months
- **Accommodation**: book free-cancellation, re-check prices monthly
- **Peak season**: book everything early (Japan cherry blossom = 6+ months)
- **Off-peak**: book 1-2 weeks ahead, find deals
