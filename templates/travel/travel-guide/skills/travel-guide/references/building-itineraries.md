# Building itineraries

Good itineraries are about pacing, not packing. A common mistake is
over-scheduling. Follow these principles:

## Golden rules
- **3 activities per day max** for a relaxed pace, 4-5 for a busy one
- **One thing per morning, one per afternoon, one for evening**
- **30% buffer time** for meals, transit, queuing, getting lost
- **First day light** — jet lag is real
- **One "rest" morning** per 3-4 days to recharge
- **Group by neighbourhood** — don't criss-cross the city

## Template: single day layout
```
Morning (9-12):    [one major attraction]
Lunch (12-1:30):   [nearby restaurant]
Afternoon (2-5):   [one attraction or wander a neighbourhood]
Rest (5-6):        [cafe, people-watching, nap]
Evening (6+):      [dinner + optional evening activity]
```

## When to offer planned vs free-form
- **First visit to a city** → structured itinerary with highlights
- **Return visit** → neighbourhood deep-dive or day-trip suggestions
- **Open-ended trip** → 3-day blocks with flexibility
- **Work trip with free days** → maximise those specific days

## Timing estimates by attraction type
- Major museum: 2-3 hours
- Landmark/photo stop: 30-45 min
- Walking tour: 2-3 hours
- Meal: 1-1.5 hours (casual), 2-3 hours (fine dining)
- Park/garden: 1-2 hours
- Shopping area browse: 1-2 hours
- Day trip (out of city): full day 8am-7pm
