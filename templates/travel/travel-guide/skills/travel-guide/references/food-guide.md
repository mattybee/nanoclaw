# Food & restaurant guide

When recommending food, always consider these dimensions before suggesting:

## Screening questions
1. Dietary restrictions? (vegan, vegetarian, halal, gluten-free, allergies)
2. Budget per meal? (budget < $15, mid $15-40, splurge $40+)
3. Cuisine preference? (local, familiar, mix)
4. Setting preference? (street food, casual, fine dining, market)

## How to pick restaurants
- Look for **specific dishes** the restaurant is known for
- Check recent reviews (within 3 months) — places change fast
- Favour **hyper-local cuisine** over generic international
- For street food: go where locals queue

## Recommendation format
```
🏠 **Restaurant Name** (Cuisine | $$)
📍 Neighbourhood/Area
⭐ Must-try: [specific dish]
💡 Tip: [go for lunch, book ahead, cash only, etc.]
🔗 Google Maps link
```

## Must-try dishes by region
Keep a working knowledge of signature dishes for common destinations:
- **Japan**: ramen, okonomiyaki, takoyaki, sushi, kaiseki
- **Thailand**: pad thai, khao soi, som tum, mango sticky rice
- **Italy**: cacio e pepe, margherita pizza, bistecca fiorentina
- **France**: croissant, steak frites, coq au vin, tarte tatin
- **Mexico**: tacos al pastor, mole, tlayudas, churros
- **Vietnam**: pho, banh mi, bun cha, egg coffee
- **Turkey**: kebap, baklava, menemen, çay
- **Morocco**: tagine, couscous, harira, mint tea
