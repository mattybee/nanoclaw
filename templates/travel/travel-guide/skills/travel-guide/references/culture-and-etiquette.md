# Culture & etiquette guide

Essential local norms by region. Flag these to the traveller when relevant.

## Temple / religious site etiquette
- **All**: cover shoulders + knees, remove shoes, speak softly
- **Buddhist** (SE Asia): don't point feet at Buddhas, don't touch monks
- **Muslim** (Middle East, SE Asia): women may need head covering
- **Hindu** (India, Nepal): remove leather items at some temples

## Tipping customs
| Country | Tipping norm |
|---------|-------------|
| USA | 15-20% restaurants, $1-2 per drink |
| Japan | No tipping (can be offensive) |
| Europe | 5-10% or round up (varies) |
| Australia/NZ | No tipping expected |
| SE Asia | 10% at nicer places, leave small change |
| Middle East | 10-15% service charge often included |

## Greetings
- **Japan**: bow (deeper = more respect)
- **Thailand**: wai (palms together, slight bow)
- **France**: "bonjour" before any request
- **Middle East**: right hand for eating, shaking, passing
- **India**: namaste (palms together)

## Dress codes
- **Southern Europe**: no beachwear outside the beach (cover up)
- **Middle East**: conservative — cover shoulders + knees
- **Japan**: modest, no pyjamas/activewear in public
- **SE Asian temples**: long pants, covered shoulders always

## Dining etiquette
- **Japan**: say "itadakimasu" before eating, don't stick chopsticks upright
- **Thailand**: eat with fork + spoon (fork pushes, spoon eats)
- **China**: leave food on plate = you're full; clean plate = you want more
- **India**: eat with right hand only
- **Middle East**: accept tea/coffee, show sole of foot is rude
- **Europe**: bread on left, water on right; keep hands visible above table

## Scams to flag
- **Tuk-tuk "free" tours** — they take you to shops that pay commission
- **Friendship bracelet "gifts"** — then demand payment
- **Counterfeit taxi dispatchers** at airports
- **"Temple is closed today"** — they offer to take you to their shop instead
- **Money changer sleight-of-hand** short-changing
